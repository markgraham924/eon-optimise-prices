OPOptimise Price Watch

Project notes

Data, privacy and terms

What is published

The public dataset contains half-hour start times, observed import and export prices, observation times and whether a slot was historical, current or forecast when collected. It deliberately excludes names, addresses, postcodes, account and site identifiers, meter identifiers, credentials, energy usage and billing information.

Where it comes from

A participating E.ON Next Optimise customer has chosen to publish the prices displayed to their authenticated account. The feed is currently labelled North West because it represents one customer site in that electricity distribution region. It should not be assumed to represent another region or every customer.

Tariff terms review

We found no clause in the Next Optimise tariff-specific terms that expressly prohibits a customer from recording or publishing their displayed prices. The terms say predicted half-hour prices are shown on a rolling basis and become fixed when delivery starts. They also describe the tariff as a public beta. This review is practical project due diligence, not legal advice or a statement that E.ON has licensed its authenticated data for republication.

Because E.ON does not currently document a public Next Optimise price API or a data-reuse licence, this project is kept non-commercial, rate-limited and privacy-scrubbed. We recommend obtaining written permission from E.ON before expanding into a large or commercial live data service.

Accuracy

The collector polls periodically, so it may miss short-lived revisions. Observation time is not supplier publication time. E.ON remains the authoritative source for a customer’s price and bill.
