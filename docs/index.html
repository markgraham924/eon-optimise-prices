About the data

Useful context before you use it

Observed from one region

This feed comes from a consenting Next Optimise customer site in the North West electricity region. It does not contain account, address, meter, usage or billing data.

Forecasts can move

Future prices are indicative. E.ON says a half-hour rate becomes fixed when delivery begins. “First seen” is our collection time, not E.ON’s publication time.

Booster included

The observed account has the Early Bird Booster, so its displayed prices include the applicable 3p import/export adjustments. Other customers may see different rates.

Independent project

This is an unofficial, non-commercial community tracker and is not affiliated with or endorsed by E.ON Next. Check your app and bill before making financial decisions.
